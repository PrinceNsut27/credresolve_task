## 2. Selection Rationale & Strategic Integration

1. **Why Better Borrower Targeting Wins**:
   - The Part 1 driver analysis proved that brute-force outreach suffers sharp diminishing returns beyond 2–3 touches (recovery falls from 7.78% to 5.08%). Better borrower targeting uses machine learning to score delinquency probability, prioritize high-cure accounts, suppress unrecoverable NPA waste, and automatically cap outreach at optimal contact thresholds.
2. **Phase-Gated Capital Guardrail**:
   - Rather than deploying ₹10 Cr blindly, we commit **₹2.50 Cr in Phase 1 (Months 1–3)** to run a 90-day 1:1 Randomized Controlled Trial across 30,000 accounts. The remaining **₹7.50 Cr (Phase 2)** is unlocked only upon achieving a statistically significant portfolio recovery uplift of $\ge +3.50\%$.
