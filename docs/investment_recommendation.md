## 4. Key Assumptions, Major Risks & Invalidation Criteria

### 4.1 Input Classification
- **`[OBSERVED]`**: Total 7-month clean recovery (₹1.268B), 30,000 master account count, attempt diminishing returns curve (7.78% at 2–3 vs 5.08% at >5).
- **`[DERIVED]`**: Annualized baseline recovery (₹217.45 Cr), targeting yield premium (+₹596/account, +₹11.13M incremental recovery), payback period (15.8 months).
- **`[ASSUMED]`**: ₹10.00 Cr capital ceiling, 12-month deployment schedule.
- **`[SCENARIO]`**: +1.50% / +3.50% / +6.00% future uplift elasticity bounds.

### 4.2 What Evidence Would Invalidate This Recommendation?
The recommendation to scale Phase 2 (₹7.50 Cr) must be **IMMEDIATELY INVALIDATED AND HALTED** if the Phase 1 RCT demonstrates any of the following:
1. **Incremental Recovery Uplift < +2.30%**: Below the 2-year break-even threshold ($p \ge 0.05$).
2. **Borrower Complaint Spike > 0.20%**: Unintended targeting aggressiveness causing customer grievance backlash.
3. **Severe Payment Reversals > 5.0%**: High chargeback rates on automated settlement commitments.
